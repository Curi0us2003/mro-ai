Getting the most out of it

  • Ask it things. "What's the torque on the main gear axle nut?" — it searches your ingested manuals and quotes the page.
  • Say identifiers naturally. Registrations, ATA chapters and part numbers are tuned for aviation speech.
  • Typing works too. Too loud in the hangar? Use the text box in the composer instead.
  • It won't invent specs. If the manuals don't cover something, it says so rather than guessing.
